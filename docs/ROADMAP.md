## Defaults and later distribution

Göttingen only, ordinary Finder folders and hourly sync by default. Never keep the Mac awake for sync. Preserve local edits, unknown files and older incoming conflicts; do not mirror remote deletion. No Finder extension or multi-university framework is planned. Developer ID/notarization, wider hardware support and broader distribution testing follow the personal release/update milestones.
